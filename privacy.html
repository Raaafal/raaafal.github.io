Privacy Policy

BUILD & RIDE, the Android game with the package name pl.favourite.drawandride
Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information BUILD & RIDE (“the game”) handles, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

In short

  • You don’t need an account. The game never asks for your name, email address, age or other contact details.
  • Your cars, progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The only ads are videos from Google AdMob that you choose to watch for a reward. To provide them, Google’s ads software in the game collects data such as your device’s advertising ID and IP address.
  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you are asked first, and you can change your choice in the game under SETTINGS → ADS PRIVACY.
  • We use no analytics or tracking services of our own, and we don’t sell personal data.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves on your device: the cars you draw (their shapes and parts), your progress, gears, achievements and garage, your settings (such as music, sounds and language), whether you have seen the tutorial, and a few dates and counters the game uses itself (for example when you last played, so it can welcome you back).

The game does not send this information to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game. If you use Android’s backup, Android may include it in your device backup in your Google Account, as it does for other apps; that is controlled by your device settings.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The only ads in the game are rewarded videos: you choose to watch one, for example to get more gears. There are no banner ads and no pop-up ads.

The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google. When the game’s main menu opens and your device is online, the game asks Google’s consent software whether a consent form applies to you and, once ads may be requested, loads one video in advance so that it is ready when you want it. A video is shown only when you choose to watch it.

According to Google, its ads software in the game (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance information;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google uses this information for advertising (including personalised ads where that is allowed), analytics and fraud prevention. More about this: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy, and the data the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects.

The ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (Google’s “PG” maximum ad content rating).

Your consent and choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form (the User Messaging Platform) asks you, before any ad is requested, whether Google and its partners may use your data, for example for personalised ads. Your choice is stored on your device. You can change it at any time in the game under SETTINGS → ADS PRIVACY; this button appears where the consent form applies. If you don’t agree, ads may still be shown, but they will not be personalised.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in the device settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Time check for the daily spin

So that the wait for the next free daily spin can’t be skipped by changing the phone’s clock, the game may ask one of these web servers for the current time: www.google.com, www.apple.com or www.cloudflare.com. The request carries no game data and no identifier from the game; the game reads only the time from the answer. As with any connection on the internet, the server receives your IP address and the technical details that every web request carries.

4. Notifications

The game can show a notification when your free daily spin is ready. Android schedules it on your device; nothing is sent to a server. You can allow or block the game’s notifications at any time in your device settings.

5. Crash reports

The game is built with the Unity engine. If the game crashes, Unity’s crash reporting may send a technical report to Unity Technologies, which makes it available to us so that we can fix the problem. Such a report describes the error (for example where in the program it happened and the game’s last log messages) and the device (for example its model and Android version). It does not contain your name, contact details or drawings. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

On Android the game uses these permissions:

  • Internet and network state: for the ads and the time check.
  • Notifications: for the daily spin reminder. On Android 13 and later they need your permission.
  • Run at startup: so that a scheduled reminder still comes after the phone restarts.
  • Advertising ID: added by Google’s ads software (see section 2).

The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or other files on your device.

7. Children

BUILD & RIDE is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. The game does not ask anyone for their age or personal details. The ads are limited to the PG rating (see section 2). If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child’s use of the game, please contact us (section 10).

8. Selling and sharing

We don’t sell or rent personal data. The only companies that receive data from the game are those described above: Google (ads, section 2), the time servers (section 3) and Unity (crash reports, section 5).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the EEA or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to or restrict its use. We don’t keep information that identifies you; for data held by Google, please use Google’s tools (your Google Account and the ads settings of your device) and the ADS PRIVACY choice in the game. Where personalised ads rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time under SETTINGS → ADS PRIVACY. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes and contact

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this policy and its effective date on this page.

Questions or requests: please open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don’t post personal information there.
