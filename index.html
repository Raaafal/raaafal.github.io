Build & Ride

Draw a car. Ride it.

Draw any car you can imagine, give it wheels and wild parts, then ride it through ramps, rockets, TNT, wobbly bridges and huge explosions – and keep its engine in one piece.

How it works

Draw it. Build it. Ride it.

Every car in the game is one you drew yourself.

  1. Step 1

    Draw

    Draw the body of your car with one finger, around its engine. A shark, a tank, a banana: whatever you draw becomes your car.

  2. Step 2

    Build

    Drag wheels onto it, then add thrusters, punches, caterpillars, a rocket launcher, and bouncy, icy or sticky parts. Keep the engine safe: if it hits anything, the car explodes.

  3. Step 3

    Ride

    Press play and watch your drawing come to life in a physics world full of ramps, rockets, TNT, springy bridges and big explosions.

Game modes

Seven ways to test your car

Each mode puts your car to a different test.

  • Adventure

    Draw the road, ride through anything

    Your drawn car rides on its own. Draw lines to bridge gaps and build ramps, and dodge rockets, boxes and gravity traps.

  • Angry Cars

    Launch your car into the towers

    Shape the ramp, set the speed, smash the towers and grab every trophy. Two cars per level: make them count.

  • Death Run

    Gas, brake, survive

    You drive, with gas and brake pedals. Jump the gulfs and flip back onto your wheels.

  • Flappy Car

    Squeeze through the columns

    Draw the terrain under the wheels. Small cars slip through, big cars get stuck.

  • Floppy Bridge

    Springy bridges, flying cars

    Build a bridge from springy parts. It bends, wobbles and bounces like a trampoline. Get three cars to the other side.

  • Battle Arena

    Build it, then watch it fight

    Design a car for the arena and its rival. The battle plays itself; the first to three points wins.

  • Explosion Run

    Draw the road, aim the launcher, fire!

    Draw the road for your car, aim your rocket launcher, and blow up the TNT and whatever blocks the way.
